Our Story

Built by a fishing family, in 2008

Kasenyi Bay started as four rooms above the family's fish store on the landing site. Two decades of guests wanting the same simple thing — a clean room, a good meal, a quiet view of the lake — turned it into thirty-two.

We still buy our tilapia straight off the boats every morning, the same boats you can see from the Bay View rooms. Half the staff grew up on this shoreline.
